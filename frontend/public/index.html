Executive Summary

What Happened? Understanding Retail Performance

Rooted strictly in descriptive analytics, the Superstore Dashboard synthesizes four years of transactional history (2015–2018) to deliver factual clarity without black-box models or speculative forecasting.

Revenue & Category Dynamics

Identify which product families and categories yield the highest retail returns. Explore how Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies contribute across 17 granular sub-categories.

Geographical Market Penetration

Analyze regional sales dispersion across the West, East, Central, and South zones, alongside state-level sales rankings that highlight top revenue-generating markets like California and New York.

Customer Segmentation & Logistics

Evaluate the contribution of Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office clients, while evaluating logistical efficiency across Standard Class, Second Class, First Class, and Same Day shipping.
